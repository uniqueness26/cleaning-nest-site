Privacy and texting terms

Cleaning Nest, a B&B Nest company. Effective November 21, 2026.

What we collect. Name, email, mobile number, ZIP code, home addresses you add, booking details, and which ad or link brought you here. For jobs, we also keep the photos taken during a clean for 90 days.

How we use it. To run your account, schedule and complete cleans, pay housekeepers, contact you about requests and jobs, and, only if you opt in, send offers.

Texts. If you check a text box, you agree to receive texts from Cleaning Nest about your account, jobs and bookings, or about offers if the box says so. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of all texts at any time, or HELP for help. Reply START to turn account texts back on. Consent to texts is not a condition of purchase or of using Cleaning Nest. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing. We do not sell your information. Mobile numbers and text consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional use. We share data only with services that help us run Cleaning Nest, such as hosting, sign-in and text delivery, and when the law requires it.

Your choices. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us. We keep a record of your text consent as proof that you agreed.

Security. Data is stored with access limits so people only see what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure.
